The first challenge was getting started. I didn't know what should I build but I did some research on how we build such systems and get to it. I chose PowerWorld for it because I felt the interface was very intuitive to work with. However, I didn't know how to work in it so I started by learning how to use it and started off with 2 bus system, then moved on to 3 bus, increased the load, saw how increasing the load changes the loading current, I also saw how reactive power increased and voltage delivered decreased due to load growth and how current compensated for it. I changed transformer tap and added parallel connection to fix those.
I gradually moved to 5 bus and did some contingency analysis to see what happens and then finally moved on to the 13-bus. 13-bus particularly as PowerWorld didn't allow me to add more than 13-bus without buying their product which I didn't want to buy. 
One of the biggest challenges was to figure out why some buses were receiving unacceptable voltage even though the lines were healthy, transformers weren't overloaded, and way below the operating limit. 
I had to look beyond equipment loading and then consider reactive power support and transformer settings for this. For example, increasing the Capacitor Bank capacitance wasn't enough so I had to change the transformer tap. 
After that, I was confused about the 15% growth scenario where voltage problems persisted. Some contingency cases still had violations that helped me identify design was still very flawed.
